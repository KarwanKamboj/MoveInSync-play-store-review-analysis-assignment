5. Data Coverage & Limitations

  • Rating vs. review sentiment: The official Google Play rating reflects all ratings, while sentiment analysis is based on the written reviews collected for the selected period.
  • Data coverage: Review coverage varies by date. Older reviews may come from sampled results, so the analysis may not represent every review in the selected period.
  • Theme overlap: A review may mention multiple problems. Issue counts can overlap and should not be added together.
  • Evidence threshold: Trends are reported only when enough relevant reviews are available; otherwise, the report states that evidence is insufficient.

Appendix B: Priority Scoring Methodology & Weights

Formula: Priority Score = 0.25*F + 0.25*S + 0.20*T + 0.15*C + 0.15*E (Scale: 1.00 to 5.00)

FactorComponentWeightScoring & Safeguard Definition
FComplaint Frequency25%1–5 scale based on theme share of focus reviews in the Primary window, capped by absolute review count (count=1 -> max 1; count=2 -> max 2; count<5 -> max 3; count<10 -> max 4) so tiny samples cannot produce high frequency scores.
SSeverity & Commuter Impact25%1–5 scale measuring journey blockage (5 = physical safety or total app launch/integrity lockout; 4 = missed pickup, auth logout, or auto-cancelled ride; 3 = prompt/support friction; 2 = UI usability). Missing review data is never interpreted as zero severity.
TRecent vs. Preceding Trend20%1–5 scale from percentage-point shift (Recent rate minus Preceding rate). Requires n >= 10 relevant reviews and >= 10 matching theme reviews per comparison window; otherwise held at neutral T=3 ('Insufficient evidence' or 'No baseline available').
CClassification & Coverage Confidence15%1–5 scale combining verified review match count, specificity/detail, and window coverage representativeness (capped at <= 2 when historical coverage is incomplete/biased or theme count < 3).
EFeasibility & Implementation Ease15%1–5 scale where higher values indicate faster engineering/operational turnaround (5 = UI modal/config change; 4 = client auth/integrity/UX fix; 3 = crash/tracking/schedule fix; 2 = multi-stop routing solver or multi-vendor ops).
  • Zero-Review Rule: Themes with 0 matching reviews receive Priority Score = None ('Unranked (0 Reviews)') and are separated into preventative monitoring guardrails.
  • Separate Safety Escalation: Credible safety incidents (T01 count > 0) are escalated on a dedicated Safety Escalation track rather than competing solely on weighted score.
  • Root-Cause Hypothesis Rule: All technical causes inferred from review text are labeled as hypotheses requiring internal product/operations telemetry confirmation.
